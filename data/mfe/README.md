MFE tracks of the released datasets are GitHub release assets (about 4-23 MB each). Download them here with

    python tools/download_mfe.py

or point `RIBOPIPE_MFE` to another directory. They are needed by models with folding energies (RiboPipe) and by
the model analyses; RiboPipe (-MFE) and the low-coverage analyses of stored predictions do not need them.
