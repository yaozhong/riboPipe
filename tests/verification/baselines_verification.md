## Notes

* fetch_patch: `fetch_upstream.sh` was run on both machines (46 and 76). Official file md5s are the ones recorded in the paper's baseline_code/*/CHANGES.md and CODE_MD5.txt. Patched-file md5s equal those recorded in the run.json `code_md5` of the paper's runs.
* smoke: tiny subsets of Mohammad16 fold 0 on 76, with cuda:0 for RiboMIMO, RiboExp and RiboGL (RiboExp/RiboGL in env ribogl_env_t1) and CPU for iXnos. The first RiboExp smoke attempt wrote MODEL_FAILED, because 1 epoch on 30 transcripts never reached the official validation interval (80 batches). Smoke mode now validates every batch; the rerun shown here passed.
* det_reproduction: Paper_CodonMean, Paper_TriCodon, Paper_Ridge and Paper_RUST were run on all 8 datasets x 5 folds from ribopipe.data (RIBOPIPE_DATA = verify_v2/data_full) and compared with the paper's runs/partI/<config>/<dataset>/seeddet/fold<f>. Every transcript is bitwise identical (max |diff| = 0).
* ixnos_cpu_rerun (extra): one full iXnos (MFE) fold was re-trained on CPU. It used the same training windows, and the per-epoch training MSE agreed to about 1e-9 relative at epoch 1. Float summation order on a loaded 64-core CPU differs from the paper run, so the 60-epoch result is not bitwise identical. Interior Pearson between new and paper predictions is >= 0.999999 per transcript. Seeded GPU methods (RiboMIMO, RiboExp, RiboGL) were not re-trained in full; they were smoke-tested only.
